At the pump, enter today's odometer as Start km and leave End km blank. The next fill-up's Start km closes this entry and works out its Km/L; the newest entry stays Pending until then. Type an End km only when you want to override that. Each row's Litres, Km/L and Cost/km use the previous fill-up's fuel; Amount is the fuel bought on that row. Use Older entry to backfill a past fill-up; tap or click any cell to edit it, Enter or tap away saves, Esc cancels.
